We are a group of developers with different backgrounds and one thing in common: a passion for WebAR and interaction design.

### Thorsten Bux

Founder of WebARKit. Software engineer with many years of experience, co-founder of ARToolKitX; he developed ARToolKitX.js, its JavaScript version, and its 2D image tracking.

[augmentmy.world](http://augmentmy.world/) · [GitHub: ThorstenBux](https://github.com/ThorstenBux)

### Walter Perdan

Co-founder of WebARKit. Visual artist deeply involved in the AR world, who develops WebAR for his artworks. He helped implement NFT in jsartoolkit5 and AR.js.

[kalwaltart.com](https://www.kalwaltart.com) · [GitHub: kalwalt](https://github.com/kalwalt)

### Daniel Fernandes

Software engineer with a special interest in CAD and AR. He developed the NFT Marker Creator, the tool used to create NFT markers.

[carnaux.github.io](https://carnaux.github.io/) · [GitHub: Carnaux](https://github.com/Carnaux)
