## Why WebARKit

We created **WebARKit** because there was a gap in the open-source world when it comes to augmented reality on the web. There are good projects — **ARToolKit5**, **ARToolKitX**, **AR.js** — but we are interested above all in markerless technologies such as **NFT** (Natural Feature Tracking) and image tracking.

We believe the open-source philosophy can achieve great results, and that accessible tools can bring augmented reality to many professionals and enthusiasts. Democratising these tools opens a technology that would otherwise remain in the hands of a few. Open source also means collaboration, a word that can open many doors.

## Why an organization

Individual open-source projects often reach a certain point and then stop, for many reasons. When the same code lives in an organization, development is much more likely to continue. That is one of the reasons we created WebARKit.

## What we work on

Image tracking first: NFT markers in the browser, compiled to WebAssembly. Around it we are building a modern computer-vision stack in **Rust** and **TypeScript** (WebARKitLib-rs, purecv, jsfeatNext), and **WebARKit Studio**, a visual node editor to create a WebAR app in a few clicks.

See the [ecosystem](/#ecosystem) for the full list of libraries and tools.

## Plan

- Make it open and easy to use.
- Invite the community to contribute and add new features.
- Discuss new features in the open.
- Establish guidelines.
- Publish a roadmap.
- Join a larger open-source foundation to reach a wider audience.
- A business model that supports open source: consulting, development support, premium features, the visual AR editor.
