---
title: FeatureSet Display
description: Visualise the feature points of an NFT marker.
---

[Open FeatureSet Display →](/tools/FeatureSet-Display/)

Upload the three files of a marker (`.iset`, `.fset`, `.fset3`) **together** and the tool draws the image with its feature points:

- light green circles: points from the FeatureSet (`.fset`)
- small red circles: points from FeatureSet3 (`.fset3`)

Points should cover the whole image. Areas without points can't be tracked on their own.
